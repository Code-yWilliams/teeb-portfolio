About

I make sure things actually get done.

For eight years I've been the person that complex, high-stakes projects depend on. I've managed a $15M+ portfolio of multi-channel initiatives — scoping projects, building timelines, coordinating across data, creative, production, and leadership teams, and making sure the work lands on time, within budget, and at the right quality bar.

My background is in marketing operations, which is one of the most demanding PM environments there is. Simultaneous workstreams, hard external deadlines, multiple vendors, senior stakeholders who need answers fast. Earlier in my career I worked directly with franchisees across 60+ locations — so I understand both sides of the client/vendor relationship.

"Colleagues have called me the velvet hammer — approachable and supportive, but firm about deadlines, quality, and follow-through."
How I Work

What I bring to every project.

01

Structure from ambiguity

I scope the work, identify owners, build the timeline, and get alignment — so execution doesn't stall while people figure out who's responsible for what.

02

Cross-functional coordination

Eight years translating between creative, data, production, and leadership. I hold projects together when each team has different priorities and languages.

03

Proactive risk management

I flag problems before they become blockers. Clear escalation paths and contingency thinking built into every plan from day one.

04

Process that sticks

SOPs, intake workflows, Smartsheet and Asana systems, leadership dashboards. Infrastructure that makes the whole team faster — not just me.

05

Budget & timeline discipline

I've owned PM tracking for $15M+ in projects. I know what slippage costs and how to prevent it without sacrificing quality or team morale.

Executive-ready communication

Clear, decision-ready status for senior stakeholders. No burying the lead — just what they need to act on.
