Process & Operations

Built a project management system from the ground up

Situation

When I joined, there was no PM system in place. Work was tracked inconsistently and leadership had no visibility into priorities or progress.

A
Action

R

Leadership gained visibility into project status they didn't have before. The team had clearer priorities each day and a consistent way to take on and track new work.

3x Systems evolved, Excel to Smartsheet to Asana, improving visibility at each stage

Segmentation & Data Strategy

Four years into acquisition success, I asked: what about the customers we already have?

S
Situation

A home services client had focused entirely on new customer acquisition for about 3 years. We had consistently hit and exceeded their goals, but I kept seeing untapped potential in the customers they already had.

A
Action

I introduced targeting known customers to upsell services and reactivate inactive ones. The trust built through consistently strong results gave me the credibility to get them to test it, even though they were hesitant at first.

R
Result

The new customer groups outperformed the existing acquisition groups by approximately 3x, successfully reactivating lapsed customers and improving RFM scores across the board. It opened up a full lifecycle dimension to their marketing strategy that hadn't existed before.

~3x Performance lift vs. acquisition groups
